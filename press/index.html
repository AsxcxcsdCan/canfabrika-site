About the studio

Can Fabrika is a one-person indie game studio based in İstanbul. It makes mobile games on two production lines: pixel-art tycoon and management games you can watch for hours, and party games you play with friends on your phones. The name is Turkish for "Can's factory": a small factory with hearts coming out of the chimney.

Games

Paw & Sword

Paw & Sword is an idle tycoon RPG where you run a town for adventurers. Build shops and training halls, recruit heroes with their own classes and personalities, send them into hunting grounds and watch them fight side by side. Gear them up, push through raids and break real boss walls. Designed to be fun to watch, not just to tap.

  • Genre: Idle, Tycoon, RPG, Pixel art
  • Platforms: iOS, Android
  • Status: In development
  • Game page

REZİL

REZİL is a party card game for adults. Each round one player is the judge, everyone else plays the funniest (or worst) answer from their hand, and the judge crowns a winner. Decks are written natively in 10 languages, including English, Turkish and German, with country-specific, expat and office decks, plus a new card drop every month.

  • Genre: Party game, Card game, Multiplayer
  • Platforms: iOS, Android
  • Status: In development
  • Age: 18+
  • Game page

HeroHaven

HeroHaven is a cozy management game about a little town of cat heroes. Build homes, shops and guild halls, send your heroes into dungeons and watch the town grow around them. Relaxed pacing, handmade pixel art and a lot of cats.

  • Genre: Management, Cozy, Pixel art
  • Platforms: iOS, Android
  • Status: In development
  • Game page
