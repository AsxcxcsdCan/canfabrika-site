REZİL: Delete your data

REZİL (Android and iOS) is published by Yusuf Demir under the Can Fabrika name. You can delete your REZİL data at any time, with or without the app.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open REZİL and tap your name or icon to open Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy & data.
  3. Tap DELETE MY DATA and confirm.

This immediately deletes your anonymous online player account (Unity Authentication) and everything stored on your phone: nickname, icon, settings, blocked players and install ID.

Without the app

E-mail admin@canfabrika.com with the subject "REZIL delete my data". If you have them, include your install ID and player ID (both shown, with a copy button, in Profile › Privacy & data). The anonymous account holds no name, e-mail or phone number, so without one of these IDs we cannot tell which account is yours. We delete your online player account and any e-mails you sent us (card ideas, reports) within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

Nothing, except where the law requires us to keep a record (for example, an e-mail about a legal claim). Card ideas that were already published in the game stay in the game without your name.

REZİL: Verilerini sil

Uygulamada (anında)

  1. REZİL'i aç, adına ya da simgene dokunarak Profil'e gir.
  2. Gizlilik ve veriler'e dokun.
  3. VERİLERİMİ SİL'e dokun ve onayla.

Anonim çevrim içi oyuncu hesabın (Unity Authentication) ve telefonundaki her şey anında silinir.

Uygulama olmadan

admin@canfabrika.com adresine "REZIL delete my data" konulu bir e-posta gönder; varsa kurulum ve oyuncu kimliğini ekle (Profil › Gizlilik ve veriler). Hesabını ve bize gönderdiğin e-postaları 30 gün içinde siler, e-postayla onaylarız.

REZİL: Daten löschen

In der App (sofort)

  1. REZİL öffnen und auf deinen Namen oder dein Symbol tippen (Profil).
  2. Datenschutz & Daten antippen.
  3. MEINE DATEN LÖSCHEN antippen und bestätigen.

Dein anonymes Online-Konto (Unity Authentication) und alle Daten auf dem Gerät werden sofort gelöscht.

Ohne App

E-Mail an admin@canfabrika.com mit dem Betreff "REZIL delete my data" (wenn möglich mit Installations- und Spieler-ID aus Profil › Datenschutz & Daten). Wir löschen innerhalb von 30 Tagen und bestätigen per E-Mail.
