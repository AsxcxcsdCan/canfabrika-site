REZİL: Help & support

Questions, bugs, a player who crossed the line, or an idea for a card? Write to admin@canfabrika.com. We usually answer within 1-2 days, and we review player reports within 24 hours.

Quick answers

How do I play with friends?

Everyone installs REZİL. One person taps HOST A PARTY, the others tap JOIN WITH CODE and type the room code. 3 or more players, or add bots.

The room dropped. What now?

The host's phone runs the room. Keep the app open on the host's phone; switching apps can drop it. Rejoin with the same code.

How do I report or block someone?

In a room, tap … at the top, pick the player, then a reason (opens a pre-filled e-mail to us) or "Just block". Blocked players can't join rooms you host.

How do I delete my data?

Profile › Privacy & data › Delete my data. More: delete your data.

Is it free?

Yes. REZİL is free with an ad between games (never during a round). A one-time "Remove ads + Party Pass" purchase turns ads off for you and for everyone in rooms you host. Bought it before? Tap Restore purchases in Settings.

REZİL: Yardım ve destek

Soru, hata, sınırı aşan bir oyuncu ya da kart fikri? admin@canfabrika.com adresine yaz. Genelde 1-2 gün içinde döneriz; oyuncu bildirimlerini 24 saat içinde inceleriz.

Arkadaşlarla nasıl oynanır?

Herkes REZİL'i yükler. Biri PARTİ KUR'a, diğerleri KODLA KATIL'a dokunup oda kodunu yazar. En az 3 kişi ya da botlar.

Birini nasıl bildirir ya da engellerim?

Odada üstteki … menüsüne dokun, oyuncuyu ve bir sebep seç ya da "Sadece engelle"ye bas.
