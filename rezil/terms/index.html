REZİL: Terms of Use

Version 2026-10-12. REZİL (Android and iOS) is published by Yusuf Demir under the Can Fabrika name. Contact: admin@canfabrika.com.

By tapping I'M 18+ AND I AGREE in the app you accept these rules. They sit alongside the store's standard licence terms (Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement on the App Store, Google Play's Terms of Service on Google Play).

1. Adults only

REZİL is a party game with crude, dark and offensive humour, made for people aged 18 and over. Don't play it, or let anyone play it, if they are under 18.

2. Zero tolerance for abuse

The jokes are on the cards, not on the other players. In nicknames, room names, card ideas and anything else you send, these are not allowed:

  • hate or harassment aimed at a real person or at people because of their origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or similar;
  • threats, sexual content involving minors, or anything illegal;
  • other people's personal data, impersonation, spam or cheating.

3. What we do about it

Names are filtered automatically. Every room has a menu (…) where you can block a player (they disappear for you and can't join rooms you host) or report them to us. We review reports within 24 hours, remove offending content, and block the player IDs of people who break these rules.

4. Card ideas

If you send us a card idea, you allow us to edit it and use it in the game for free. We credit you by nickname only if you ask us to.

5. The game as it is

REZİL is provided as it is, and online rooms depend on third-party services that can be unavailable. Nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under the law of your country.

6. Changes

If these rules change in a way that matters, the app asks you to accept them again.
