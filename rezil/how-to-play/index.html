How to play REZİL

A practical guide to the card rounds, room settings and Android closed test.

REZİL is a text-based party card game for adults aged 18 and over. Players combine a written prompt with answers from their hand, then choose a winning combination. Humour comes from the combinations people choose. The game includes mature humour; an age label is not a promise that every joke suits every group.

Access to the Android closed test

REZİL is currently in closed testing on Google Play. It is not yet a public Android release. Access depends on your Google account being eligible for the test: opening a store link alone does not add you to the tester group.

  1. Contact admin@canfabrika.com if you need help joining the test.
  2. With an eligible Google account, open the Google Play test opt-in page and follow Google's instructions.
  3. Use that same account in Google Play to install the available build. If you see an unavailable message, check the selected Google account and tester eligibility first.

As of 17 October 2026, the Android closed test is on version 1.1.0, build 4. This guide describes that release's rules. Future builds may change the card catalogue or controls.

Start a table

The host creates a party and shares its room code. Friends choose Join with code on their own phones and enter the same code. A table supports 3 to 10 players, including bots. You can add bots when you have too few people, or use Play with bots to learn the round flow before hosting friends.

Choose the card language and decks before starting. REZİL has cards in English, Turkish, German, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Brazilian Portuguese and Hinglish. The menu language and the cards selected for a room serve different purposes: agree on a language everyone at the table understands.

Play a round

A pink prompt card appears. Read its blank spaces and choose an answer from your hand that completes the sentence. A Pick 2 prompt needs two answer cards; select them in the order you want them read. For example, a prompt about the perfect first date could combine “a group project” with “a fire alarm”. These are illustrative examples from the in-app tutorial.

In Rotating judge, one player sits out the answer selection, reads the submitted combinations and picks the winner. The judge changes each round. In Democracy, everyone votes and you cannot vote for your own submission. A tied vote is resolved with a coin flip.

Scoring and game modes

A round win normally earns one point. The host can enable extra rules that change scoring. Crowd Pick awards an extra point for the crowd's favourite, and Underdog adds a point for winning from last place. Read the room settings before assuming every round is worth the same amount.

  • Classic: play toward the target score selected by the host.
  • Speed: play toward a target score with timed turns.
  • Blitz: the highest score when the game clock ends wins.
  • Unlimited: keep playing until the host ends the game.

Classic and Speed use a target of 3 to 10 points. In timed modes, the game can play for a player whose turn expires. Under More options, hosts can enable Judge's Choice, Crowd Pick, Twists and the Underdog bonus. Twists include double points, reverse, lightning and hand swaps. Touch grass lets you replace your whole hand once per game.

Room etiquette and player controls

Use a nickname that is safe to share with other players. Keep jokes within the game and respect real people. In a room, open the player's … menu to report or block them. A blocked player's name is hidden and they cannot join rooms you host. For a problem that prevents using those controls, contact support with the version number and a description of what happened.

Ads, purchases and your data

The game is designed to show interstitial ads between games rather than during a round. Ad availability depends on the build, consent and the advertising service. The optional one-time Remove ads + Party Pass purchase removes ads for the owner and provides an ad-free room when that owner hosts. Check the price shown by the store before purchasing; use Restore purchases when recovering an existing purchase.

Open Privacy & data in the game to review advertising choices or request deletion of your data. Online rooms use an anonymous Unity player account, even though you do not need to create a username and password. Deleting local app storage alone is different from deleting that online account. See the privacy policy, data deletion help and terms for details.

If you lose a connection or a purchase does not appear, keep the version number and error text, check your connection, and contact REZİL support. Never send passwords or payment-card details with a support request.
