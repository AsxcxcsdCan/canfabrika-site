Support

Help for all Can Fabrika games on iPhone, iPad and Android. Türkçe destek de veriyoruz.

Contact

Email admin@canfabrika.com. We usually reply within 48 hours, in English or Turkish.

Reporting a bug

Please include the game name, your device model, the iOS or Android version, the game version (shown in the game's settings) and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Purchases and refunds

  • Restore purchases: open the game's settings and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Refunds on iPhone/iPad: Apple handles all refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Refunds on Android: Google handles refunds through Google Play.

Ads and privacy

To stop personalised ads, see the "Your choices" section of our privacy policy. To have any data linked to you deleted, email admin@canfabrika.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name of the game.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Our games work without an account, email or phone number.

Is REZİL suitable for children?

No. REZİL is an adult party game for players aged 18 and over.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

Progress is saved on your device. Write to us with your device details and we will help where we can.
