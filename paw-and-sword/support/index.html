Paw & Sword: Help & support

Questions, a bug, or lost progress? Write to admin@canfabrika.com, or use Menu › About › Contact support in the game (it adds your game version and device for us). We usually answer within 1-2 days.

Quick answers

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, automatically. Menu › Settings › Save now saves right away. If your version has cloud save, a copy is also kept online (see the Cloud save line in Settings).

I reinstalled and my town is gone.

Uninstalling deletes the save on the phone. If cloud save was on, open Menu › Settings and tap Sync now. Otherwise write to us with the player code from your old install, if you have it.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you tap an ad button for a bonus. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Change your ad privacy choice in Menu › About › Privacy choices (ads) where shown.

A purchase did not arrive.

Restart the game while online. On iPhone use Menu › About › Restore purchases. If it is still missing, write to us with the receipt date (not your payment details).

How do I turn off notifications?

Menu › Alerts, or your phone's notification settings.

How do I delete my data?

See delete your data.

Paw & Sword: Yardım ve destek

Soru, hata ya da kaybolan ilerleme? admin@canfabrika.com adresine yaz ya da oyunda Menu › About › Contact support düğmesini kullan. Genelde 1-2 gün içinde döneriz.

İlerlemem nerede kaydediliyor?

Telefonunda, otomatik olarak. Sürümünde bulut kayıt varsa bir kopyası çevrim içi de tutulur (Menu › Settings).

Reklam izlemek zorunda mıyım?

Hayır. Reklamlar yalnızca bir bonus için reklam düğmesine dokunduğunda oynar.

Verilerimi nasıl silerim?

Verilerini sil sayfasına bak.
