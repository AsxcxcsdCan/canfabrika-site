Paw & Sword: Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-12. This policy covers the Paw & Sword app for Android and iOS. The Can Fabrika website has its own privacy notice.

Short version

  • No account needed. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or location.
  • Your town is saved on your phone.
  • Optional cloud save keeps a copy of your town with Unity Gaming Services under an anonymous player ID.
  • Video ads appear only when you tap an ad button for a reward. No banners, no ads that interrupt play. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before any ad is loaded.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own. We do not sell your personal data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Some features (cloud save, ads, purchases, notifications) may not be switched on in every version or country. This policy describes everything the game can do; a feature that is not in your version processes no data.

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

Yusuf Demir (publisher of Paw & Sword under the Can Fabrika name)
E-mail: admin@canfabrika.com

2. What data the app uses, and why

a) Stored only on your phone

Your town and progress (heroes, items, buildings), settings, backups of earlier towns (made when you start a new town or load a cloud save), and a small technical log file (errors, game version, device model; two files of about 512 KB at most, overwritten as it grows). This data never leaves your phone unless you use cloud save (b) or send it to us yourself.

b) Cloud save (Unity Gaming Services)

If cloud save is on in your version, the game signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication and stores in Unity Cloud Save (Unity Technologies SF, 30 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA, and its affiliates): a random player ID, a copy of your save file and its version number. Your IP address is processed to connect. If you choose Play on another device and link a Unity account, Unity also processes the e-mail address or sign-in method of that account. Purpose: back up your town and restore it on another device. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you use). Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal.

c) Rewarded video ads (Unity LevelPlay)

Ads are shown only when you tap an ad button (for example to double a reward). They are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with for this game. To show, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may process: your advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), the IDFV / app set ID, device and connection data (model, operating system, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it), and which ads were shown, tapped or completed.

Consent. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) after start-up, and no ad is loaded until the form allows it. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree; if you decline, ads may still be shown without personalisation. You can change your answer at any time in Menu › About › Privacy choices (ads). On iOS the system asks whether the game may track you only when you start your first ad; your answer never changes the reward, and you can change it in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised ads; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in non-personalised ads and fraud prevention. Partner policies: Unity, Google.

d) Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) through Unity IAP. We never see your payment details. The app receives a receipt (product, time, transaction ID) to deliver and restore what you bought. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

e) Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example that your heroes are back) are scheduled locally on your phone. No push server is used. Turn them off in Menu › Alerts or in your phone's settings.

f) Support e-mails

Menu › About › Contact support opens your own e-mail app with a message to admin@canfabrika.com, pre-filled with the game version, device model and operating system. We receive what you send and use it only to answer you. Our e-mail runs on Google Workspace (Google Ireland Ltd.). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

g) No analytics, no selling

We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we do not sell personal data. Ad partners may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out as described in section 5.

3. Transfers and how long we keep data

Unity, Google and the ad partners may process data outside your country, including in the USA, on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Cloud save and anonymous player ID: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support e-mails and requests: up to 12 months, unless we need them longer to handle a legal claim.
  • Ad partners and stores keep data as described in their own policies.

4. Children

Paw & Sword has cartoon fantasy content suitable for a general audience, but the app is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and the ad networks are told that it is not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, up to 16 in parts of the EU). Younger players should ask a parent before agreeing to personalised ads. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, write to us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 15-21 and 7(3) GDPR), and the rights under Turkish law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 11). In California and other US states you can ask what is collected, ask for deletion, and opt out of "sharing" for targeted ads: use Privacy choices (ads) where shown, or turn on "Limit ad tracking" / "Delete advertising ID" (Android) or deny tracking (iOS). To use your rights, write to admin@canfabrika.com. For cloud data, include the player code shown in Menu › Settings under Cloud save. You may complain to your local data protection authority, or in Türkiye to the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu.

6. Delete your data

See canfabrika.com/paw-and-sword/delete-data.

7. Security and changes

The game sends data only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We will post changes on this page and update the version date at the top; for important changes we will also tell you in the game.

Paw & Sword: Gizlilik Politikası

Sürüm 2026-10-12. Bu politika Android ve iOS için Paw & Sword uygulamasını kapsar. Oyunun menüleri İngilizce olduğu için menü adlarını İngilizce yazdık.

Kısaca

  • Hesap gerekmez. Adını, e-postanı, telefonunu ya da konumunu asla istemeyiz.
  • Kasaban telefonunda kaydedilir.
  • İsteğe bağlı bulut kayıt, kasabanın bir kopyasını anonim bir oyuncu kimliğiyle Unity Gaming Services'te saklar.
  • Video reklamlar yalnızca ödül için bir reklam düğmesine dokunduğunda gösterilir. Banner yok, oyunu kesen reklam yok. AB, AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de herhangi bir reklam yüklenmeden önce onayını isteriz.
  • Satın almaları Apple ya da Google yürütür; ödeme bilgilerini asla görmeyiz.
  • Kendi analiz ya da takip aracımız yok. Kişisel veri satmayız.
  • Oyun 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir.

Bazı özellikler (bulut kayıt, reklamlar, satın almalar, bildirimler) her sürümde ya da ülkede açık olmayabilir; sürümünde olmayan bir özellik veri işlemez.

1. Veri sorumlusu

Yusuf Demir (Paw & Sword'u Can Fabrika adıyla yayınlayan)
E-posta: admin@canfabrika.com

2. Hangi veriler, neden

Yalnızca telefonunda: kasaban ve ilerlemen, ayarların, önceki kasabaların yedekleri ve küçük bir teknik kayıt dosyası (hatalar, sürüm, cihaz modeli). Bulut kayıt kullanmadıkça ya da bize kendin göndermedikçe telefonundan çıkmaz.

Bulut kayıt (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Technologies, ABD): rastgele bir oyuncu kimliği, kayıt dosyanın bir kopyası ve sürüm numarası; bağlantı için IP adresin işlenir. Play on another device ile bir Unity hesabı bağlarsan Unity o hesabın e-posta adresini ya da giriş yöntemini de işler. Hukuki dayanak: GDPR m. 6(1)(b), KVKK m. 5/2-c.

Ödüllü video reklamlar (Unity LevelPlay ve birlikte çalıştığı reklam ağları): reklam kimliği (Android) ya da izin verirsen IDFA (iOS), IDFV / uygulama seti kimliği, cihaz ve bağlantı verileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, IP adresi ve buna dayalı yaklaşık konum) ve hangi reklamların gösterildiği. AB/AEA/Birleşik Krallık/İsviçre'de Google'ın onay formu gösterilir ve form izin verene kadar reklam yüklenmez; kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar yalnızca onay verirsen gösterilir. Seçimini Menu › About › Privacy choices (ads) altından değiştirebilirsin. iOS izleme sorusu yalnızca ilk reklamı başlattığında gelir ve cevabın ödülü değiştirmez.

Satın almalar: Apple ya da Google tarafından Unity IAP üzerinden işlenir; yalnızca bir makbuz (ürün, zaman, işlem kimliği) alırız.

Bildirimler: telefonunda yerel olarak planlanır, push sunucusu yok. Menu › Alerts ya da telefon ayarlarından kapatabilirsin.

Destek e-postaları: Menu › About › Contact support kendi e-posta uygulamanı sürüm, cihaz modeli ve işletim sistemi bilgisiyle açar. Yalnızca sana cevap vermek için kullanırız (Google Workspace).

Unity, Google ve reklam ortakları verileri ABD dahil yurt dışında işleyebilir (AB-ABD Veri Gizliliği Çerçevesi ve/veya standart sözleşmeler).

3. Saklama süreleri

Telefondaki veriler: uygulamayı kaldırana ya da depolamasını temizleyene kadar. Bulut kayıt ve anonim oyuncu kimliği: silinmesini isteyene kadar. E-postalar: en fazla 12 ay.

4. Çocuklar

Oyunun içeriği genel kitleye uygun çizgi film tarzı fantastik bir içeriktir, ancak uygulama 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir ve reklam ağlarına çocuklara yönelik olmadığı bildirilir. 13 yaş altı çocuklardan bilerek veri toplamayız; böyle bir durum fark edersen bize yaz, sileriz.

5. Hakların

KVKK m. 11 ve GDPR m. 15-21 kapsamında bilgi talep etme, düzeltme, silme, itiraz, onayı geri çekme ve diğer hakların var. admin@canfabrika.com adresine yaz; bulut verileri için Menu › Settings içinde Cloud save altında görünen oyuncu kodunu ekle. Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu'na ya da kendi ülkendeki veri koruma otoritesine şikâyette bulunabilirsin.

6. Verilerini sil

Paw & Sword: Datenschutzerklärung

Stand 2026-10-12. Gilt für die App Paw & Sword (Android und iOS). Einige Funktionen (Cloud-Speicher, Werbung, Käufe, Benachrichtigungen) sind nicht in jeder Version oder jedem Land aktiv; eine nicht enthaltene Funktion verarbeitet keine Daten.

1. Verantwortlicher

Yusuf Demir (Herausgeber von Paw & Sword unter dem Namen Can Fabrika)
E-Mail: admin@canfabrika.com

2. Verarbeitete Daten

Nur auf deinem Gerät: Stadt und Spielfortschritt, Einstellungen, Sicherungen früherer Städte und eine kleine technische Logdatei (Fehler, Version, Gerätemodell).

Cloud-Speicher (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Technologies, USA): zufällige Spieler-ID, Kopie des Spielstands und Versionsnummer; die IP-Adresse wird zur Verbindung verarbeitet. Bei Verknüpfung eines Unity-Kontos (Play on another device) verarbeitet Unity auch dessen E-Mail-Adresse bzw. Anmeldemethode. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO.

Belohnte Videowerbung (Unity LevelPlay und dessen Werbenetzwerke): nur, wenn du auf eine Werbe-Schaltfläche tippst. Verarbeitet werden ggf. Werbe-ID (Android) bzw. IDFA (iOS, nur mit deiner Erlaubnis), IDFV / App-Set-ID, Geräte- und Verbindungsdaten (Modell, Betriebssystem, Sprache, IP-Adresse und daraus abgeleiteter ungefährer Standort) und Werbeinteraktionen. In EU/EWR, Großbritannien und der Schweiz erscheint vorher das Einwilligungsformular von Google (UMP); ohne Freigabe wird keine Werbung geladen, personalisierte Werbung nur mit Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO), sonst nicht personalisierte Werbung und Betrugsabwehr (lit. f). Widerruf jederzeit unter Menu › About › Privacy choices (ads).

Käufe: Abwicklung durch Apple bzw. Google über Unity IAP; wir erhalten nur einen Beleg (Produkt, Zeit, Transaktions-ID), keine Zahlungsdaten.

Benachrichtigungen: nur lokal auf dem Gerät geplant, kein Push-Server.

Support-E-Mails: Die App öffnet deine Mail-App mit Version, Gerätemodell und Betriebssystem. Nutzung nur zur Beantwortung (Google Workspace, Google Ireland Ltd.).

Keine eigenen Analyse- oder Tracking-Werkzeuge, kein Verkauf von Daten. Übermittlung in die USA auf Grundlage des EU-US Data Privacy Framework bzw. von Standardvertragsklauseln.

3. Speicherdauer

Gerätedaten bis zur Deinstallation oder Löschung des App-Speichers; Cloud-Spielstand und Spieler-ID bis zu deinem Löschantrag; E-Mails höchstens 12 Monate.

4. Kinder

Die App richtet sich nicht an Kinder unter 13 Jahren; den Werbenetzwerken wird mitgeteilt, dass sie nicht kindgerichtet ist. Wir verarbeiten wissentlich keine Daten von Kindern unter 13.

5. Deine Rechte

Auskunft, Berichtigung, Löschung, Einschränkung, Datenübertragbarkeit, Widerspruch und Widerruf (Art. 7 Abs. 3, 15-21 DSGVO) sowie Beschwerde bei einer Datenschutz-Aufsichtsbehörde. Anfragen an admin@canfabrika.com, für Cloud-Daten mit dem Spielercode aus Menu › Settings (unter „Cloud save“).
