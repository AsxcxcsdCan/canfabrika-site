HeroHaven: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. This policy covers the HeroHaven app for iPhone, iPad and Android. The Can Fabrika website has its own privacy notice.

1. Who we are

HeroHaven ("the game", "we", "us") is made by Yusuf Demir, trading as Can Fabrika (sole proprietorship), Veysel Karani Mah. Gülhane Cad. No: 7E, Sinpaş GYO E Blok, İç Kapı No: 87, Sancaktepe / İstanbul, Türkiye. E-mail: admin@canfabrika.com. We are the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. The short version

  • You can play as a guest. We never ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • The game works offline. Your town is saved on your device.
  • If you sign in (Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games), your town is backed up to our cloud save so you can restore it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are collected only if you agree. You can change your choice any time in Settings.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your data in the game (Settings > Delete Account / Delete My Data).

3. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

We do not process special categories of data, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ids, or browsing history.

4. Service providers (processors)

We use these providers under data processing agreements. They process data only on our instructions: - Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Analytics, Remote Config, Diagnostics), USA / EU. Privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. - Apple and Google process payments, sign-in and app distribution as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. - Our e-mail host, which stores support e-mails sent to admin@canfabrika.com.

Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA, for example in the USA. We rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. No tracking, no ads, no sale of data

We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If an advertising SDK is added later, this section, the in-app consent and the store privacy labels will be updated before that version is released.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Usage statistics: Settings > Options > "Share anonymous usage data" ON/OFF. Turning it off stops collection from that moment.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Delete Account (or Delete My Data as a guest). Without the app: canfabrika.com/herohaven/delete-data. This deletes your online account, your cloud save and the local town on this device. You can also ask us at admin@canfabrika.com or through the support page; please include your Player ID from Settings. Purchase records that tax law requires us to keep, and the records Apple or Google keep, are not affected. One-time purchases can be restored from your Apple/Google account.
  • Your GDPR rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and withdrawing consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to admin@canfabrika.com.
  • Complaint: you may complain to the data protection authority where you live (in the EU, your national or regional authority; in Türkiye, the KVKK at kvkk.gov.tr).

7. Children

HeroHaven is rated for ages 9+ (App Store) / PEGI 7 (Google Play) but is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what the game needs to run (section 3). Usage statistics are only collected with consent. In Germany, a person under 16 may consent only with a parent's approval (Art. 8 GDPR). If you believe a child gave us personal data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local saves stay in the app's private storage on your device.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when the game changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced in the game.
