Privacy Policy

This policy covers the website canfabrika.com and all Can Fabrika games for iPhone, iPad and Android (Hero Heaven Tycoon, HeroHaven, REZİL and future titles). Türkçe özet · Deutsch

1. Who is responsible

Yusuf Demir, sole proprietorship trading as Can Fabrika. Email: admin@canfabrika.com. This is the data controller under the EU/UK GDPR and the "veri sorumlusu" under Turkish law no. 6698 (KVKK).

2. The website

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). To deliver pages, GitHub processes your IP address, date, time and the page requested in server logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads through Google AdSense (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. If you agree, ads may be personalised based on your interests.

  • Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent banner before any personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy and cookie settings" link in that banner. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com or opt out of third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG) for personalised ads; legitimate interest for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention.

Things we do not do on the website

No analytics or tracking tools of our own, no social media plugins, no Google Fonts requests (fonts are served from our own server). Your browser only stores, on your device, whether you closed the language hint.

3. Our games

What the games collect

Our games do not ask for your name, email or phone number and do not require an account. Depending on the game, the following data is processed:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): some free games show ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Online multiplayer (REZİL): rooms run on Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies (Unity Authentication, Multiplayer Services and Relay). The game signs you in anonymously with a random player ID (no name, email or password), and processes the nickname you choose, the room code, your IP address for the connection, and the game data needed to run the match. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal.
  • Purchases: payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made.
  • Engine statistics: the Unity engine sends Unity Technologies basic, anonymous device information (such as device model, operating system and graphics chip). We use no other analytics or crash-reporting service.

Your choices in the games

  • iPhone and iPad: we ask through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads. You can reopen the choice from the game's settings menu.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13. REZİL is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to admin@canfabrika.com and we will delete it.

4. Email

If you email us, we use your message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Email is provided by Google Workspace. We delete messages when they are no longer needed, at the latest after 3 years, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

5. International transfers

Google, Unity and GitHub may process data in the USA and other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention periods.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, and object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR; Art. 11 KVKK). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To use these rights, or to ask us to delete any data linked to you, email admin@canfabrika.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (kvkk.gov.tr), in the EU the authority of your country.

8. Changes

We update this policy when our games or services change. The date below shows the latest version.
