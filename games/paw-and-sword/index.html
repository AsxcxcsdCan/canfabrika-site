About the game

Paw & Sword is an idle tycoon RPG where you run a town for adventurers. Build shops and training halls, recruit heroes with their own classes and personalities, send them into hunting grounds and watch them fight side by side. Gear them up, push through raids and break real boss walls. Designed to be fun to watch, not just to tap.

  • A compact, busy town: heroes live, shop and gossip
  • Heroes hunt together in battles that are fun to watch
  • Classes, weapons, meaningful loot, raids and real boss walls
  • Plays offline · iOS and Android

Questions

Is Paw & Sword like Evil Hunter Tycoon?

Yes. It shares the fun-to-watch town and hunting loop, with more classes, monsters, hunting grounds, raids and bosses.

Does it play offline?

Yes. The core game plays fully offline on iPhone and Android.
