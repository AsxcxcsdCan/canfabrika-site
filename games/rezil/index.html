About the game

REZİL is a party card game for adults. Each round one player is the judge, everyone else plays the funniest (or worst) answer from their hand, and the judge crowns a winner. Decks are written natively in 10 languages, including English, Turkish and German, with country-specific, expat and office decks, plus a new card drop every month.

  • Everyone joins from their own phone with a room code
  • 10 languages, country decks, expat and office decks
  • No friends around? Play with bots
  • Free · no ads mid-game

Questions

Is there a Turkish Cards Against Humanity?

Yes: REZİL has decks written from scratch in Turkish, including expat (gurbetçi) and office (beyaz yaka) decks.

Do all players need the app?

Yes. Everyone joins from their own phone with a room code, so there are no cards to pass around.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Brazilian Portuguese and Hinglish.
