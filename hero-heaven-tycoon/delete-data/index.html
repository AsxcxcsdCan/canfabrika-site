Hero Heaven Tycoon: Delete your data

Hero Heaven Tycoon (Android and iOS) is published by Yusuf Demir under the Can Fabrika name. You can delete your Hero Heaven Tycoon data at any time.

On your phone

Uninstall the game. This removes your town, settings, backups and the log file. On Android you can also keep the app and use Settings › Apps › Hero Heaven Tycoon › Storage › Clear storage.

Note: Menu › Start a new town in the game starts over but keeps your old town as a backup on the phone. It does not delete your data.

Cloud save (by e-mail)

  1. In the game, open Menu › Settings and note the player code shown under Cloud save (for example "Player 1a2b3c4d").
  2. E-mail admin@canfabrika.com with the subject "Hero Heaven Tycoon delete my data" and that player code.

We delete your cloud save, your anonymous Unity player account and any e-mails you sent us within 30 days, and confirm by e-mail. If you no longer have the game, write anyway and tell us roughly when you last played; we will do what we can to find your data.

Ads

We do not hold ad data ourselves. Change your ad choice in Menu › About › Privacy choices (ads) (where shown), reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and see the ad partners' policies linked in the privacy policy.

What is kept

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google as the law requires. We keep nothing else, except where the law requires a record (for example an e-mail about a legal claim).

Hero Heaven Tycoon: Verilerini sil

Telefonunda

Oyunu kaldır: kasaban, ayarların, yedekler ve kayıt dosyası silinir. Android'de Ayarlar › Uygulamalar › Hero Heaven Tycoon › Depolama › Depolamayı temizle de kullanılabilir. Not: oyundaki Menu › Start a new town eski kasabanı yedek olarak saklar; verileri silmez.

Bulut kayıt (e-postayla)

  1. Oyunda Menu › Settings'i aç, Cloud save altında görünen oyuncu kodunu not al.
  2. admin@canfabrika.com adresine "Hero Heaven Tycoon delete my data" konulu bir e-posta gönder ve kodu ekle.

Bulut kaydını, anonim Unity oyuncu hesabını ve bize gönderdiğin e-postaları 30 gün içinde siler, e-postayla onaylarız. Satın alma kayıtları yasa gereği Apple ya da Google'da kalır.

Hero Heaven Tycoon: Daten löschen

Auf dem Gerät

Spiel deinstallieren (Android: auch Einstellungen › Apps › Hero Heaven Tycoon › Speicher › Speicher löschen). Menu › Start a new town behält die alte Stadt als Sicherung und löscht nichts.

Cloud-Speicher (per E-Mail)

Spielercode aus Menu › Settings (unter „Cloud save“) notieren und an admin@canfabrika.com mit dem Betreff "Hero Heaven Tycoon delete my data" senden. Wir löschen Cloud-Spielstand, anonymes Unity-Konto und deine E-Mails innerhalb von 30 Tagen und bestätigen per E-Mail. Kaufbelege bewahren Apple bzw. Google gesetzlich auf.
