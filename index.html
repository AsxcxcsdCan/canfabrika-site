About the factory

I'm Can. I live in Berlin, make games and tell stories on YouTube. Can Fabrika is where all my games come from: a small factory with hearts coming out of the chimney. Every game is built with the same care.

Two production lines: pixel-art games you can watch for hours, and party games for playing with friends. More are on the way.

Is there a game like Evil Hunter Tycoon?

Yes: Paw & Sword. The same fun-to-watch town and hunting loop, with more classes, monsters, raids and bosses.

REZİL! Decks written from scratch in 10 languages including Turkish, plus expat and office decks.

All three are in development for iOS and Android. Send an email to hear first.
