About the factory

I'm Can. I live in Berlin, make games and tell stories on YouTube. Can Fabrika is where all my games come from: a small factory with hearts coming out of the chimney. Every game is built with the same care.

Two production lines: pixel-art games you can watch for hours, and party games for playing with friends. More are on the way.

Yes: Hero Heaven Tycoon. The same fun-to-watch town and hunting loop, with more classes, monsters, raids and bosses.
