Whisker Hollow: Delete your data

Whisker Hollow: Hero Town 1.0 saves your town on your iPhone. It does not create an online game account or cloud save.

In the game

Open Settings > Danger zone > Delete My Data and follow the confirmation steps. This deletes local progress, saves and backups, local identifiers and error logs. Town progress and consumed items cannot be recovered through Restore Purchases.

Without the app

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its local app data. Can Fabrika cannot remotely erase a town stored only on your device. If you previously sent a support e-mail or report, contact admin@canfabrika.com to request deletion of that correspondence.

Purchase records

Deleting local game data does not cancel or erase Apple's transaction records. Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored with the same Apple Account. Consumable Moonstones and bundles cannot be restored after consumption; restoring purchases does not restore your town.
